Research

Tailored Attention Mechanism for Radar Point-Cloud Generation on Embedded GPUs
A. Hernandez, A. Pegatoquet, J.-Y. Dauvignac, R. Christy-Krishanth, A. Fakhfakh · Submitted to IEEE RadarConf 2027 (Bangalore, May 2027)

Instead of convolving the whole radar cube (15.4 million bins, over 99% of them noise), a radar tokenizer keeps about 30,000 candidate cells, then an asymmetric-attention transformer decides which ones form the point cloud.

My contribution: co-design and implementation of the model, ONNX → TensorRT FP16 deployment on Jetson, latency and energy measurement protocol, paper writing.
